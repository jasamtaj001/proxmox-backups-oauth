Proxmox Backups

A private backup utility for its owner's Proxmox infrastructure.

When enabled, it copies completed local backup archives to the owner's Google Drive. The copies are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload. Their contents and filenames are decrypted only with the owner's recovery configuration.

Google authorization is limited to files created by this application or explicitly selected for it. This is a personal-use tool, not a public backup service. It does not offer account registration or access to anyone else's files.
